AI Solution

AI-Based Road Pothole Detection is an AI solution for the public-sector roads and transport industry. It applies deep learning and computer vision to photographs of road surfaces: a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), trained on 1,157 labelled images, decides whether a photograph shows a pothole or a normal road, reports its confidence and recommends the next action. The system is delivered as a Python application with a Streamlit interface (app.py) in which an official uploads a road image, receives the result in about a second, and keeps a downloadable detection history. It answers the theme “an AI Solution for Industries” and the brief’s call for 4IR technology that benefits people on the ground, because damaged roads affect every resident and every municipality, and AI can screen road images faster and more consistently than manual inspection alone. On the 136-image test set supplied with the project the model reaches 83.82% accuracy and finds 95.4% of the potholes; a leakage-free retraining of the same network is reported in the Model Evaluation section.
